I build software for healthcare, where a broken release affects real people. That shapes how I think about engineering: stability, security and quality come before speed.

Gen AI is changing how we write code. I write here about what changes, what stays the same, and how engineers can keep delivering software that works for the people who use it.

What I believe

  • The role was never the code. The role is to deliver working software.
  • Ship better, not more. Time saved by AI should become value and quality, not volume.
  • Guardrails make speed safe. Tests, security scans and checks in CI pay off on every change.
  • Keep learning both halves: the new tools and the old fundamentals.
